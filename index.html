Optical TMM - What it measures

Reflection, transmission, absorption and parasitic losses per layer of a solar cell across a spectrum.

Model outputs

  • Spectral Reflection / Absorption / Transmittance and (layer‑resolved) absorpance
  • External Quantum Effiiency (EQE) style photocurrent estimation via integration
  • Identify limiting layers + loss channels

Use in design

  • Compare to measured devices to validate stack assumptions
  • Guide thickness/material changes with quantified impact
  • Accelerate iteration by narrowing lab experiments
